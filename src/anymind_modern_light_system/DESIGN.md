## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, ultra-clean enterprise commerce aesthetic. It takes visual cues from multi-faceted business operations—global supply chain, cross-border e-commerce, digital marketing, and automated logistics. The tone is authoritative yet dynamic, combining deep corporate navy authority with the kinetic energy of primary and secondary spectrum accents (vibrant blue, dynamic crimson red, warm gold yellow, and fresh growth green).

The design movement blends **Corporate Modernism** with **Vibrant Functionalism**:
- Pristine, high-luminance canvas layouts that emphasize data legibility, structured grids, and crisp spatial rhythm.
- Purposeful chromatic accents derived from the brand emblem, functioning systematically as categorization markers, analytical indicators, and high-impact micro-interactions without visual fatigue.
- High-contrast precision typography that delivers immediate legibility for mission-critical operations, real-time analytics, and enterprise inventory management.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an immaculate light foundation, grounded by deep AnyMind Navy and punctuated with calibrated brand spectrum accents:

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#ffffff`)**: Pure white background for maximal contrast, clean space, and clear structural hierarchy.
- **Surface Muted (`#f8fafc`)**: Soft cool-slate background used for page backdrops, sidebars, and nested data cards.
- **Surface Subdued (`#f1f5f9`)**: Subtle structural contrast used for table headers, active tab containers, and button hover states.
- **Border Default (`#e2e8f0`)**: Delicate, razor-sharp perimeter lines separating functional zones.
- **Border Strong (`#cbd5e1`)**: Focused states, segmented dividers, and interactive container outlines.

### Brand Anchor & Typography
- **Primary Navy (`#0f172a`)**: Deep, commanding navy used for primary headlines, high-impact buttons, and critical status bars.
- **Navy Secondary (`#1e293b`)**: Body text, table rows, and secondary header labels.
- **Slate Text (`#64748b`)**: Secondary annotations, metadata, breadcrumbs, and placeholder iconography.

### Semantic & Emblazon Accents
- **Vibrant Blue (`#2563eb`)**: Primary action indicators, links, active navigation tabs, and system process counters.
- **Dynamic Red (`#e11d48`)**: Urgent notifications, system errors, negative delta indices, and critical stop actions.
- **Energetic Yellow / Gold (`#f59e0b`)**: Warnings, pending inventory states, attention badges, and highlight tags.
- **Fresh Growth Green (`#10b981`)**: Positive yields, successful confirmations, healthy network statuses, and live sync badges.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages **Inter** for its neutral geometry, high x-height, and legible rendering across data tables, executive summaries, and multi-channel metrics.

- **Headlines & Display:** Tightly tracked with negative letter spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) and strong weights (`700` and `800`) to mirror the sturdy brand wordmark.
- **Body:** Calibrated line heights (`1.5rem` on standard body) allow multi-column tables and narrative updates to breathe while maintaining scanning speed.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Enhanced weights (`600` and `700`) paired with subtle positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.05em`) for tags, status indicators, table headings, and badges.
- **Tabular Numerics:** All metric displays and table data should invoke `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'cv05' on` to keep numbers vertically aligned across rapid updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a **12-column fluid grid** centered on an 8px architectural unit:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px to 48px canvas margin. Max container width caps at 1440px for focused administrative dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 24px margins. Cards collapse from 4-up to 2-up configurations; side navigation defaults to an icon-rail.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, and 16px (`1rem`) margin. Grids collapse into vertical single-column stacks with edge-to-edge scrollable carousels for dense data chips.

Component density maintains balanced interior margins (`space-md` for standard card padding, `space-sm` for compact utility bars), balancing information richness with visual clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, translucent ambient shadows combined with structural borderlines to retain an ultra-clean, clinical feel:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Used for master page canvases (`#f8fafc`) and base table rows. Flat, defined purely by `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Resting elevation for content tiles, KPI monitors, and data containers. Background is `#ffffff`, framed with a `1px solid #e2e8f0` border and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Context)**: Applied to interactive cards on hover, segmented tooltips, and floating filter bars. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dropdowns)**: Menus, contextual dialogs, select flyouts, and drawer panels. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Command Center)**: Centered blocking modals and system notifications. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, layered over a scrim of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The geometric character employs **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to balance modern tech approachable curves with enterprise precision:

- Standard controls (inputs, buttons, select menus) use an outer border radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- Structural containers (cards, data tables, modals) use `rounded-lg` at `0.75rem` to `1rem` (12px–16px).
- Status tags, pill badges, and segmented toggles utilize full rounded caps (`9999px`) to echo the circular geometry of the brand emblem.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Navy:** Background `#0f172a`, text `#ffffff`, border none. Hover: `#1e293b`. Focus ring: `2px solid #2563eb` with a 2px white offset.
- **Brand Accent Action:** Background `#2563eb`, text `#ffffff`. Hover: `#1d4ed8`. Used for direct call-to-actions (e.g., "Create Campaign", "Launch Shipment").
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#0f172a`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Hover: background `#f8fafc`, border `#cbd5e1`.
- **Tertiary Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#64748b`. Hover: background `#f1f5f9`, text `#0f172a`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical padding, `space-sm` horizontal padding, paired with `label-sm` uppercase styling.
- **Growth (Success):** Background `#ecfdf5`, text `#065f46`, border `1px solid #a7f3d0`.
- **Alert (Danger):** Background `#fff1f2`, text `#9f1239`, border `1px solid #fecdd3`.
- **Attention (Pending):** Background `#fffbeb`, text `#92400e`, border `1px solid #fde68a`.
- **Info (Active Blue):** Background `#eff6ff`, text `#1e40af`, border `1px solid #bfdbfe`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Height: 40px standard. Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, font `body-md`.
- Focus state: border color `#2563eb`, outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- Helper text in `body-sm` `#64748b`. Error state transforms border to `#e11d48` and focus ring to `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkbox: 18px × 18px, `rounded` (4px). Radio: 18px × 18px `rounded-full`.
- Default: border `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`, background `#ffffff`.
- Checked: background `#0f172a` (or `#2563eb` depending on module focus), icon white, border `#0f172a`.

### Cards & Analytical Containers
- Pure white background (`#ffffff`), `1px solid #e2e8f0`, `rounded-lg` (12px), padded with `space-lg` (24px).
- KPI metric headers incorporate an accent top border (2px height) or mini badge reflecting the relevant sector color (blue, red, yellow, or green) to provide instant visual indexing across complex multi-brand operations.

### Data Tables
- Header row: background `#f8fafc`, text `#64748b`, typography `label-md` uppercase, border bottom `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- Data row: background `#ffffff`, hover background `#f8fafc`, border bottom `1px solid #f1f5f9`, typography `body-sm`. Numeric columns right-aligned with tabular figures enabled.
